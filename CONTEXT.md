# Apprentice Cursus

Grade tracking and training portfolios for an apprenticeship program, where supervisors follow the apprentices they are responsible for.

## Language

### People

**Apprentice**:
A member of an apprentice group in Entra ID, who records their own grades and portfolio. Known to the app whether or not they have signed in yet.
_Avoid_: Student, learner, apprenti (outside UI copy)

**Supervisor**:
A Trainer or Coach who can read and comment on the Apprentices they supervise.
_Avoid_: Mentor, manager

**Trainer**:
A Supervisor who supervises every Apprentice in their Apprenticeship.
_Avoid_: Formateur (outside UI copy), teacher

**Coach**:
A Supervisor who supervises only the Apprentices assigned to them, across Apprenticeships.

### Structure

**Apprenticeship**:
The program an Apprentice follows (IT or EC); it decides their grade tree.
_Avoid_: Section, track, filière (outside UI copy)

### Account state

**Deactivated**:
An Apprentice who no longer belongs to an apprentice group; their history stays readable but nothing about them can change.
_Avoid_: Deleted, archived, removed
